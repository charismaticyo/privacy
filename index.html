Privacy Policy

Auralis Auto Gallery • Effective Date: October 12, 2026

1 Overview & Introduction

Welcome to Auralis Auto Gallery ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with our website, applications, automated integrations, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) services (collectively, the "Services").

We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining transparent practices in full compliance with the Meta Platform Terms, Developer Policies, and applicable privacy laws.

2 Information We Access & Process

When you connect our automation tools or applications to your Facebook Page via the Meta Graph API, our application may access the following authorized data strictly based on permissions granted by you:

  • Page Information: Page name, ID, category, fan count, and public profile details.
  • Post & Content Data: Posts, captions, uploaded media (images/videos), scheduling status, and permalinks.
  • Engagement & Comments: Comments, comment replies, reactions, and public user display names associated with comments on your Page.
  • Insights & Analytics: Aggregate metrics including impressions (organic, paid, unique), engagement count, like totals, and click statistics.

Note on Personal User Accounts: Our services connect strictly to Facebook Pages via authorized Page Access Tokens. We do not access, store, or monitor private personal profile data, personal friend lists, or private personal messages.

3 How We Use Your Information

We process the accessed data solely to provide the specific automation and management features you trigger, including:

  • Publishing and scheduling posts to your Facebook Page upon your direct command.
  • Reading and replying to comments or questions from your audience.
  • Moderating comments (filtering spam, hiding inappropriate content, deleting abusive remarks).
  • Generating operational summaries and performance metrics for your Page content.

We never sell your data, lease it to third parties, or use it for cross-site behavioral advertising.

4 Data Storage, Retention & Security

Our automation tools and MCP servers operate locally on your designated environment or secure server instance. Access tokens and environment credentials (such as FACEBOOK_ACCESS_TOKEN and FACEBOOK_PAGE_ID) are stored locally in secure environment configurations.

  • Ephemeral Processing: Data fetched from the Graph API is processed in-memory for your active session and is not archived on centralized external databases.
  • Token Security: API keys and access tokens must remain confidential and should never be committed to public repositories.

5 Facebook Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with the Meta Platform Developer Terms, users and Page administrators have the right to request deletion of any data associated with their usage of our applications.

How to revoke access and request data deletion:

  1. Log in to your Facebook account and navigate to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. In the left sidebar, click Security and login or Business Integrations / Apps and Websites.
  3. Locate the Auralis Auto Gallery application and click Remove.
  4. To request manual deletion of any remaining cached records, submit an email to privacy@auralisautogallery.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" along with your Facebook Page ID.

Requests are processed and fulfilled within 30 days of receipt.

Our services integrate with Meta's Graph API. Use of Facebook and Meta features is governed by the Meta Terms of Service and Meta Privacy Policy.
